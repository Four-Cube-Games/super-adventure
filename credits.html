Credits

Wildwalk is built on other people's work. The places you walk through are drawn from tile packs made by artists who sell or share them, its battles lean on open data, and it couldn't have been made without open-source software. Thank you to every one of them.

Places and tiles Characters Interiors Music Data Software and fonts About AI

The game draws its own pictures from these packs, a tile at a time. The packs themselves stay with us: what reaches you is only the pictures the game draws from them. Options in the game has a Credits screen listing them too.

Places and tiles

Cute Fantasy RPG

by Kenmi

The backbone of the outdoors: towns, routes and cities, their grass, paths, cliffs, water, trees and houses. Its Volcano, Desert and Christmas add-ons dress the volcano, desert and snow stretches, and its pieces turn up in hideouts, gyms and the Elite Four's halls.

Ninja Adventure

by Pixel-Boy and AAA

Caves and tunnels, the snow underfoot, and a second look for towns and routes.

The Fan-tasy Tileset, with its Desert Oasis and Snow Adventures add-ons

by Valerio Colonna (Ventilatore)

A third look for towns, cities and routes, and the desert's sandstone towns and palm woods.

Pixel Crawler

by Anokolisa

Everywhere the mood darkens: villain hideouts (foundries, markets, labs and cellars), the haunted tower, every gym's arena, the formal gardens of gym districts. From the base pack and its Castle, Forge, Library, Cemetery, Garden, Hideout, Sewer, Cave, Desert and Fairy Forest add-ons.

Pixel Art Top Down - Basic

by Cainos

The legendary shrines: mossy stone ruins on raised ground, their pillars, statues and the dais a legend sleeps on.

Characters

Cute Fantasy RPG and its Characters add-on

by Kenmi

Everyone who walks: your trainer, built in layers from the pack's player so you can dress how you like, and the trainers, townsfolk, nurses, clerks and guards you meet.

Interiors

Cozy Interior

by shubibubi

The insides of buildings: every Center, Mart and special building, furnished whole, from the plank floors and wallpaper to the counters, shelves and the PC.

The Fan-tasy Tileset: Medieval Interiors

by Valerio Colonna (Ventilatore)

The insides of townsfolk's houses.

The Fan-tasy Tileset: Castles and Fortresses

by Valerio Colonna (Ventilatore)

The Elite Four's castle: the gatehouse and forecourt, a court for each member climbing tier by tier, and the Champion's keep.

Testers

Thank you to the players who tried the early builds, found what broke and told us about it: sammysenrab1 and blainesarcanine. Their long, thoughtful feedback on the rules, the pace and how the game should feel shaped much of what's here.

About AI

Wildwalk is made with a lot of help from Claude. Every picture you see comes from the human artists credited above; none of the art is AI-generated. The worlds aren't AI either: they're built by hand-written rules from a random seed, the way roguelike dungeons are, and nothing in the game calls an AI while you play.

Made something we use and don't see your name here? Please tell us and we'll put it right.
