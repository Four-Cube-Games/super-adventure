A creature-collecting RPG for Discord

Catch them with your whole server.

Super Adventure is the classic Red and Blue games, played inside Discord. Every server gets its own region, generated from scratch: new routes, new towns, gyms of random types, and rare creatures hiding somewhere different every time. Everyone explores it together, one stretch of map a day, racing to be first into the next town.

Add it to your server Read the handbook

A map nobody has seen

Eight gyms, villain hideouts, legendary shrines and an Elite Four, laid out fresh from a random seed. Each world is one region, from Kanto to Paldea, with that game's creatures and moves. Your server votes on the next one.

Played together

The whole server shares one world. You can open one new stretch a day, and the first trainer into a town opens the way for everyone else. There's no grinding someone else's lead away, and no falling hopelessly behind.

Battles you remember

Types, stats, status moves, switching and the original catch formula, with the later games' best bits: abilities, natures, weather, critical hits. Gym leaders and champions switch to suit the fight.

Catching is the game

An unlimited box, one shiny in 1,024, rares that only exist in some worlds, a Safari-style preserve, and legends that sleep at shrines or roam the late wilderness.

Friends, and rivals

Trade, challenge a friend's team or battle them live in the channel, bring down a weekly raid boss together, and climb the leaderboards for your server and the whole bot.

A reset is a graduation

Seasons last two to six weeks, at a pace your server picks. When one ends, the world you played becomes your own private one, to finish whenever you like.

How to play

There's no map to walk around: you play with slash commands and buttons, and your screen is private to you, so a whole server can play in one channel without spamming it. Type /start to join your server's world, or send it in a direct message to start a world of your own.

Servers are approved by hand while the game is young. Add the bot, and you'll hear back by direct message. Want the newest features first? Ask to join the beta.

Something you'd change? Every rule in the handbook has a feedback button, and everything sent lands on the public feedback board.
