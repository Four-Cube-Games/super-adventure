A creature-collecting adventure inside Discord

Walk a world nobody has seen before.

Wildwalk is an adventure you walk through inside Discord, with your whole server. Every server gets its own region, built from a seed: its own towns and routes, caves and cliffs, gyms of random types, and rare creatures hiding somewhere different every time. Every season the world is made anew, so nobody, not even us, knows the map before you walk it. Open it in a channel and you're there, tile by tile, with your friends walking about beside you.

A world made for your server

Eight gyms, villain hideouts, legendary shrines and an Elite Four, laid out fresh from a seed. Towns grow their own streets, routes have cliffs, rivers and the sea, and each stretch has its own look, from meadow to snow to volcano. When the season ends, a whole new world takes its place, and your server votes on its region.

Walk it, tile by tile

Tall grass rustles, creatures wander the routes in plain view, and trainers spot you from across the path. Every Center, Mart and gym has an inside, and gyms have puzzles: ice to slide on, crates to push, switches, warp panels and dark mazes.

Together, live

Everyone in the same place walks about with you, live, names over their heads. The roads are open, and the server's big places open together, one a day: a gym, a hideout, a shrine, and the Elite Four last. Walk up to another player to battle them, trade or see their card.

Battles you remember

Animated, with a 16-bit soundtrack. Types, natures, abilities, weather, critical hits and switching, and gym leaders and champions who switch to suit the fight and keep their ace for last.

Catching is the game

An unlimited box, the odd shiny, rods to fish with, rares that only exist in some worlds, a fenced preserve, and legends that sleep at shrines or roam the late wilderness.

Friends, rivals, and a reset that's a graduation

Trade, battle a friend live, bring down a raid boss together, and climb the leaderboards. Won the Hall of Fame? Begin again for a prestige star. When a season ends, the world you played becomes yours to finish whenever you like.

How to play

Type /play or /activity in any channel of a server that has the bot, and the adventure opens right there in Discord, on a computer, in a browser or on your phone. The first time, you choose your partner from three. Walk with the arrow keys or the on-screen pad, and the start menu holds your party, bag, dex, map, trainer card, wardrobe and options. Trades, battles and raids happen in the world, face to face, and the leaderboard is a slash command in your server.

Servers are approved by hand while the game is young. Add the bot, and you'll hear back by direct message. Want the newest features first? Ask to join the beta.

Something you'd change? Every section of the rules has a feedback button, and everything sent lands on the public feedback board.
